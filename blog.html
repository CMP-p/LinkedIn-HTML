Why Dogs Are Superior

10/3/2026 11:54

I'd really like to retract what was said in an earlier blog post. While cats are indeed cute little fuzzy creatures, and go way back in time, they are not the superior species.

I would first like to mention that dogs were also recorded in ancient egyption history. A mask of a dog or wolf would be worn on the head of a prominent leader. Wolf packs are always often known to have a leader. But it's more than that. The social hierarchy of dogs is one that -if respected- leads to a functioning and thriving community. Dogs know their place and operate to their capacity within. They aim to protect the community and are loyal to one another.

While loyatly is a great point, it's more than that. Love from a dog can be felt. Imagine your dog at home, or someone who does have one. When interacted with, you can tell if a dog is happy, excited or sad. When it's time to eat, he recognizes that you're the one who feeds him and gets excited at your offering. If you decide to put the food away instead, he will be sad, but will still love you. If you decide to go for a walk instead, he'll forget you ever put the food away, and be excited to go outside and get some air and excercise.

So yes, while cats are strong and independant cute creatures, dogs are loyal, loving and respectful creatures. They don't use their strength in isolation to win, they use their strength as a community.
