About Me

I like to think of myself as someone who's got a lot of mind and not a lot of structure. So much mind in fact, that putting it into a structure would cause my mind to probably be hurt. I'm not exactly trying to say I'm the greatest person who ever lived, the next Jesus, or even a great thinker. Well, actually, no. I think I'm a pretty great thinker. I just don't think I'm the greatest the world has ever seen. I try to stay humble. It sure is isolating, though. When you know and undertand the folly of the world, but the world still lives in it. I am imperfect myself, anyways. So ofcourse I understand, that being brought your shortcomings, and hearing to them, is something that must be overcame willfully, not forcefully. Not most of the times anyways.
