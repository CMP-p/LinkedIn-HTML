About Me

I like to think of myself as someone who's got a lot of mind and but not yet a lot of structure. So much mind in fact, that putting it into a structure is causing my mind to be hurt. I'm not trying to say I'm the greatest mind that's ever lived, the next Jesus, Gahndi, MLK, or even a great thinker. Well... actually... no. I think I'm a pretty great thinker, I just don't think I'm the greatest the world has ever seen, or even close. I'd prefer to stay humble. It can be isolating, though. For example, when you know and understand some of the follies of the world, yet watch the world continue in it. To see a correction that'd lead to a greater outcome be ignored based on some odd sense of pride and ego. It is corruption. I, however, am imperfect myself. I therefore of course understand, that being brought your shortcomings and hearing to them, is common to man. It is something that must be entered into and overcame willfully, not forcefully. Atleast, most of the times anyways.
